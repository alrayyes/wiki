# anteroom

1. An outer room that opens into another room, often used as a waiting room.
2. A smaller room before a chief apartment, to which access is had through it; especially, a waiting-room used for the temporary reception of visitors, etc.; an antechamber.
3. A room before, or forming an entrance to, another; a waiting room.
