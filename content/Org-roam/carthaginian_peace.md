# Carthaginian peace

A Carthaginian peace is the imposition of a very brutal "peace" achieved by
completely crushing the enemy.The term derives from the peace imposed on
\[\[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient\_Carthage]\[Carthage]] by \[\[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient\_Rome]\[Rome]]. After the \[\[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second\_Punic\_War]\[Second Punic War]], Carthage lost all its colonies,
was forced to demilitarize and pay a constant tribute to Rome and could enter
war only with Rome's permission. At the end of the \[\[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third\_Punic\_War]\[Third Punic War]],the Romans
systematically burned Carthage to the ground and enslaved its population.
